Why Process Matters More Than Tools

Most AI projects fail—not because the technology doesn't work, but because there's no clear process, no ownership, and no plan.

You've seen it: companies buy AI tools, get excited for a week, then abandon them because no one knows how to use them or integrate them into actual workflows.

We don't work that way. Every engagement follows a structured, five-phase process designed to minimize risk, maximize ROI, and ensure your team actually adopts what we build.

Every phase has clear deliverables, timelines, and success criteria. No ambiguity. No surprises.

1

Phase 1: Discover & Audit

We map your operations, interview your team, and identify where AI and automation will deliver the biggest impact.

What Happens:

  • • Stakeholder interviews with your team
  • • Process mapping of current workflows
  • • Opportunity analysis and ROI ranking
  • • Quick win identification

What You Get:

  • • Detailed audit report
  • • Prioritized opportunity list
  • • ROI estimates
  • • 60-minute results presentation

Timeline: 1–2 weeks

What We Need from You: 3–5 hours of interview time, access to workflow documentation, examples of current pain points

2

Phase 2: Design the Roadmap

We turn audit findings into a prioritized, actionable 30-90 day plan with clear costs, timelines, and expected outcomes.

What Happens:

  • • Initiative prioritization based on ROI
  • • Technical architecture design
  • • Cost & timeline estimation
  • • Risk assessment

What You Get:

  • • 30-60-90 day implementation roadmap
  • • Detailed project briefs
  • • Tool and vendor recommendations
  • • 90-minute strategy walkthrough

Timeline: 2–3 weeks

What We Need from You: Budget/timeline constraints, feedback on priorities, access to current tech stack

3

Phase 3: Build & Integrate

We design, build, test, and deploy your custom AI and automation solutions—while keeping your existing systems running smoothly.

What Happens:

  • • Solution design (wireframes, specs)
  • • Development of integrations & workflows
  • • Staging & testing
  • • Production deployment with monitoring
  • • Documentation creation

What You Get:

  • • Fully functional AI/automation systems
  • • Integrations with existing tools
  • • Complete documentation
  • • Staging environment access
  • • 30-day post-launch support

Timeline: 4–12 weeks (depending on scope)

What We Need from You: System access, timely feedback on designs, designated point person, QA participation

4

Phase 4: Train & Enable

We don't just hand you a system and disappear. We train your team to use it, troubleshoot it, and evolve it over time.

What Happens:

  • • Hands-on training workshops
  • • Role-based training
  • • Recorded walkthroughs
  • • Office hours for Q&A

What You Get:

  • • Live training sessions
  • • Recorded video guides
  • • Written user guides & FAQs
  • • Follow-up support access

Timeline: 1–2 weeks (concurrent with/after build)

What We Need from You: Team participation, feedback on materials, willingness to practice

5

Phase 5: Optimize & Evolve

We monitor performance, gather feedback, and continuously improve the system as your business grows.

What Happens:

  • • Performance monitoring
  • • Feedback collection from your team
  • • System refinement based on data
  • • Feature expansion as you scale

What You Get:

  • • Monthly performance reports (if on retainer)
  • • Proactive optimization recommendations
  • • Bug fixes and updates
  • • Strategic guidance on next opportunities

Timeline: Ongoing (optional retainer)

What We Need from You: Honest feedback, usage data, openness to iteration

Your Role vs. Our Role: Clear Expectations

What We Need from You

  • • Time for discovery: 3–5 hours of interviews during Phase 1
  • • Access to systems: Logins, API keys, tools we'll integrate
  • • Timely feedback: Review designs and answer questions promptly
  • • A point person: One team member to coordinate
  • • Honest communication: Tell us when something isn't working

What We Handle

  • • All technical work: Design, development, testing, deployment
  • • Project management: Timelines, milestones, communication
  • • Training & enablement: Workshops, guides, videos, Q&A
  • • Ongoing optimization: Monitoring, improvements, scaling
  • • Risk mitigation: Testing, careful deployment, rollback plans

You focus on running your business. We handle the AI and automation.

How We Minimize Risk

We know you're worried about things breaking, data getting lost, or wasting money on something that doesn't work. Here's how we de-risk every engagement:

We test in staging first

Nothing goes live until you've reviewed and approved it in a safe test environment.

We integrate, we don't replace

We work with your existing tools—no rip-and-replace projects unless absolutely necessary.

We document everything

If we get hit by a bus, you still have full documentation and can hand it to another dev.

We prioritize quick wins

You see ROI early, usually within the first 30 days, so you're not waiting months to see value.

We build with rollback plans

If something goes wrong, we can revert quickly without data loss.

Track record matters

We've never lost client data, broken a critical system, or disappeared mid-project.
